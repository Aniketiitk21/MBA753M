# Causal Inference Methods for Business Analytics

## Mentor  
Prof. Nivedita Bhaktha, Dept. of Management Science  
Jul 2024 – Sept 2024

## Project Overview  
Applied causal inference techniques to analyze economic policies and demographic factors affecting employment and wages.

## Key Contributions  
- Conducted **Difference-in-Differences (DiD)** analysis to study the impact of minimum wage changes on employment.  
- Performed **Event Study** analysis to evaluate the effects of petrol price policies across Indian states.  
- Investigated the relationship between **Gross National Income (GNI)** and demographic variables using regression, addressing multicollinearity issues.  
- Applied **Instrumental Variable (IV) regression** to resolve wage endogeneity and extract meaningful policy insights.
